1. Information We Process

AYUSHMAAN VENTURES respects your privacy. The app com.ayushmaan.dailypresent (Daily Present App) does not require account registration and does not transmit your personal employee records or salary numbers to any cloud server operated by us.

However, to support the app through advertising, third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may automatically collect limited device and interaction data as described in Section 4 below.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All business information entered into the application—such as employee profiles, base salary rates, daily attendance statuses, custom weekly offs, overtime logs, and cash advance records—is stored strictly within a local SQLite database on your device. This data remains under your control unless you explicitly export or share a backup or PDF salary slip.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Storage Access Framework: Used only when you intentionally choose to export/import an offline backup or share a PDF salary slip.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

This application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (including banner ads). Google and its advertising partners may collect and use pseudonymous data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)
  • App interactions, ad impressions, and diagnostic/crash data

This information is used to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and measure ad performance. Your private employee attendance and salary records are never shared with advertisers.

Opt-Out & Controls: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads anytime in your Android phone via Settings → Google → Ads.

For more details on how Google uses data, please read the Google Advertising Privacy Policy and Google Privacy & Terms.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can edit or delete any employee, attendance, or advance entry inside the app at any time.
  • You can delete all app data via Settings → Clear All Data or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for workforce attendance and payroll calculation and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
